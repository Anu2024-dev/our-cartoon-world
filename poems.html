1. The Cartoon World

A world of colours, a world of fun,
Where every day feels like the sun.
Funny friends and stories bright,

2. My Blue Friend

A little blue cat from far away,
With magic gadgets every day.
Helping friends whenever they fall,
Doraemon is loved by all!

3. Never Give Up

Dream so high and reach the sky,
Keep on trying, always fly.
Even when the road is tough,
Believe in yourself, you are enough!
