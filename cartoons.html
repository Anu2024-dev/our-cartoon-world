Our Favourite Cartoons

1. Doraemon

Doraemon is a robotic cat from the future. He helps Nobita with his amazing gadgets.

Genre: Comedy, Adventure, Science Fiction

2. Naruto

Naruto is a young ninja who dreams of becoming the strongest leader of his village.

Genre: Action, Adventure

3. Shinchan

Shinchan is a funny little boy who always makes everyone laugh with his silly actions.

Genre: Comedy, Family

4. Pokemon

Pokemon follows Ash and his friends as they travel, make friends and train Pokemon.

Genre: Adventure, Fantasy

5. Tom and Jerry

Tom and Jerry are famous for their funny chases and endless mischief.

Genre: Comedy, Animation
