Did You Know?

Who created Doraemon?

Doraemon was created by the Japanese manga artist duo Fujiko F. Fujio.

What is Naruto's dream?

Naruto dreams of becoming the Hokage, the leader of his village.

What type of Pokemon is Pikachu?

Pikachu is an Electric-type Pokemon.

Who are Tom and Jerry?

Tom is a cat and Jerry is a mouse. They are famous for their comic rivalry.

What is special about Shinchan?

Shinchan is known for his funny behaviour and mischievous personality.
